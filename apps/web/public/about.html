Chat

Wallet messaging and connected email from Bittrees.

Chat brings encrypted wallet direct messages, community conversations and an optional connected Mail inbox into one app. Connecting Mail requires an eligible mailbox and your permission; shared sign-in does not grant mailbox access or room membership.

Operated by Chat Admin. Contact chat@bittrees.org for service questions.

Public preview

Chat is still in public preview. Managed rooms depend on the organization’s configured access service and membership rules. Automatic email-to-wallet forwarding is not generally enabled. A stable native release is not yet available.

Messages, mailbox contents, account settings and administration are private app features, not public search content. Never share recovery phrases, private keys or authentication tokens.
